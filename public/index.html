    Unofficial, community-run and not affiliated with NEA, HSA or NParks. Locations can change — the sign on the ground always wins. You must be 21+ to buy or use tobacco in Singapore.

    Know the rules first

    Where you can't smoke

    Singapore bans smoking in over 32,000 places. As a rule of thumb: if it has a roof, is used by the public, or is a queue, a bus stop, a lift lobby, an overhead bridge, a covered walkway or within 5 m of most building entrances — don't.

    Since 1 Oct 2022 that also covers all public parks, gardens, beaches and reservoirs (the red areas on the map).

    The red areas are not the whole story. They're drawn from NParks' published boundaries, so land run by someone else — Gardens by the Bay, Marina Barrage, Sentosa, HDB void decks, private grounds — shows up blank even though smoking is banned there too. An empty map is not permission.

    The Orchard Road No-Smoking Zone

    Since 1 Jan 2019 the whole of Orchard Road is a No-Smoking Zone. Inside the amber outline, smoking is an offence everywhere except inside one of the 52 yellow-box Designated Smoking Areas — the official pins on this map, each with NEA's own photo of the box.

    What it costs

    A composition fine of S$200, or up to S$1,000 if you're taken to court. Littering a cigarette butt is a separate offence starting at S$300.

    Age & vapes

    The minimum legal age for buying, using or possessing tobacco is 21. E-cigarettes and vapes are completely illegal in Singapore — possession alone is an offence, and none of the shops on this map may legally sell them.

    Why isn't this just Google Maps?

    Because Google won't let it be. Google's Maps Platform terms forbid storing their Places content or drawing it on a non-Google map — and this map is built on OneMap and OpenStreetMap. So nothing here is scraped from Google, and every spot links straight out to Google Maps instead, live, where the reviews and photos actually live.

    Use both: this map for the official lists and the rules, Google for opening hours and whether the place still exists.

    Where the spots come from

    NEA publishes the 52 Orchard yellow boxes. Changi Airport Group publishes 32 more across the four terminals — the only other operator in Singapore that publishes a list at all. Everything else is OpenStreetMap contributors: a handful of mapped smoking areas, plus 61 hawker centres, coffee shops, bars and offices that someone tagged as having a smoking corner.

    That last group is the least reliable thing on this map. Nobody re-checks it, so treat it as a lead, not a promise.

    How complete is this?

    The 52 official smoking areas are NEA's full published list — they only exist inside the Orchard Road zone. Elsewhere in Singapore, smoking corners at coffee shops, offices and industrial estates are set up by the premises owner and there is no national register, so those come from OpenStreetMap contributors and are far from complete. Spot one that's missing? Drop a note on OpenStreetMap and it'll flow into this map on the next rebuild.

    The 4,000+ shops are every outlet holding a current HSA tobacco retail licence — that's the legal answer to "who can sell me cigarettes", though opening hours and whether they're still trading aren't in the register.
